Privacy and data

Mirror reads the active tab's address once a minute to sort it into a category (short video, social, news, other), then discards it. In demo mode it does this every 2 seconds. It also reads the tab title, and on YouTube the video's own category (such as Education or Gaming), to tell studying from time-wasting. Sites, addresses, titles and categories are never stored or uploaded; the popup shows only the kind of activity. When you waste time, Mirror draws its nudge as a card on top of the page, which pauses any video. It can do this only on the time-wasting sites listed below, and it reads nothing from them. Chrome describes these permissions as "Read your browsing history" and "Read and change your data" on those sites.

Sync is optional. If you connect an account, daily totals are encrypted here before upload, and the policy below allows only our sync server. The server still sees your email, IP address and when you sync. The current two recovery words are for the prototype, not strong protection.

The camera is off unless you turn on posture. The optional face check keeps drowsiness, focus, restlessness and, only if you switch it on, the expression the camera saw (not your feelings). Frames are read in memory on this laptop and never saved or sent. Only proportions, the last result, recent blink rates and daily estimates are kept.
