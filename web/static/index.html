Privacy and data

    
The server sees your email, IP address, when you sync, and how many records you have. It does not receive in readable form dates, minutes, sleep or device types. Sites and app names are never uploaded.

    
Prototype note: two recovery words are enough for a demo, not for strong protection. Real accounts will use more.
